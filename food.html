Run-day fuel

Long run (10 mi)

Before

  • Big banana + 250 mL water, about 30–45 min before
  • Extra 100–150 mL water at the start

During

  • Small banana at ~45–50 min
  • 2 dates at ~80–90 min
  • 150–200 mL fluid every 20 min, pinch of salt

After (30–45 min)

  • Protein scoop with milk + banana or dates
  • Then a proper meal with carbs and protein

No protein or fat-heavy food during the run. It digests slowly and can upset the stomach.

Overnight infused water

  • Cucumber slices, 2 lemons, 1 orange, 1 amla
  • 1–2 spoons sugar or honey (honey: use less, stir in last)
  • Pinch of salt on run days

Tips

  • Lemon peel and pith turn bitter, so remove after 4–6 h or squeeze in just before
  • Acidic: use a straw, rinse with water after, wait 30 min before brushing
Fish at home

Quick masala fish

  1. Marinate 10 min: salt, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp chilli powder, lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste
  2. Heat a non-stick tawa with 1 tsp oil
  3. Cook 3–4 min per side until golden
  4. Finish with lemon. Serve with rice or roti and salad

Which fish

  • Mild, few bones: rawas (Indian salmon), pomfret, surmai (kingfish), basa
  • Most omega-3: bangda (mackerel), tarli (sardines). Stronger smell
  • Skip before runs: deep-fried rava fry

Best after a run or at dinner. Avoid within 3–4 h before a run. Aim for 2–3 times a week.

Less smell

Before cooking

  • Buy fresh: clear eyes, red gills, no strong odour. Fillets or steaks
  • Soak 10 min in cold water with lemon or a spoon of vinegar
  • Pat fully dry, then marinate with turmeric, salt, lemon, ginger

Cooking

  • Air fryer / oven: 200°C, 8–10 min, foil on the tray (least smell)
  • Steam: in banana leaf or foil, 10–12 min
  • Pan: non-stick, exhaust on, window open

After

  • Boil lemon peels in water, or leave a bowl of vinegar out
  • Wash pan and hands with lemon and salt

What to order

  • Day before a long run: varan-bhaat with ghee, bhakri with a vegetable and dal, simple thali
  • After a run: pithla-bhakri, moong or sprouts usal, curd or sol kadhi, egg or paneer bhurji with roti
  • Skip the night before: misal, vada pav, deep-fried items
